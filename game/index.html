☄️ Comet Courier

Pilot a courier ship. Pick up a glowing parcel, deliver it to the station of the same colour, dodge asteroids, and grab star fuel. Complete 5 deliveries before the timer runs out.

Move: WASD / Arrow keys  •  Boost: click the canvas (costs fuel, aims at the click)  •  Pause: P  •  Restart: R  •  Sound: M (off by default)
